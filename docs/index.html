What it is

Vice Daily Shorts is a private publishing tool used by one creator (The Vampire Path) to run their own YouTube channel about news for the upcoming video game GTA 6. It turns public gaming news articles into short vertical news videos with an AI voiceover, captions and infographics, and uploads them to the creator's own YouTube channel.

How it works

  1. Reads public RSS news feeds about GTA 6.
  2. Writes a short original news script and records a voiceover.
  3. Edits a 30–60 second vertical video using official Rockstar Games press-kit media, original graphics and captions.
  4. Sends the finished video to the owner on Telegram for review.
  5. After the owner approves, uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel with videos.insert.

Use of YouTube API Services

The tool only calls youtube.videos.insert (about 3 uploads a day) on the single channel owned by the operator, authorised once with Google OAuth 2.0. It does not read, store or display any other user's YouTube data, has no public sign-up and no other users.

By using YouTube features through this tool, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
